The opportunity

Skill is built, not credentialed.

Most people trying to break into tech aren't short on effort or ambition — they're short on structure and honest feedback. Tutorials get you started; they rarely get you reviewed. Tech Impact Fellowship exists to close that gap: a defined weekly path, practical work that mirrors how real teams operate, and a mentor whose job is to tell you the truth about your work and help you improve it.

What you get

Everything that turns effort into progress.

A real curriculum, not a link dump

Learning tracks broken into courses, weekly modules and lessons — each week has a clear objective, estimated time, and a practical task that proves you got it.

A mentor who actually reviews your work

Every practical task submission gets read and reviewed — approved, or sent back with specific feedback. Weekly huddles keep you accountable and unstuck.

Practical work, not busywork

Ship real, working things — with a GitHub repository and a live demo, the same artifacts you'd point a hiring manager to.

A cohort, not a solo grind

You move through the programme alongside a cohort of peers on the same schedule — same weekly rhythm, shared momentum, real accountability.

A portfolio worth sharing

Your approved projects build into a public portfolio — publish it when you're ready, on your own terms.

A certificate that means something

Earned, not handed out — tied to real completion, attendance and approved work, with a mentor's and administrator's sign-off. See exactly what it takes below.

How it works

From application to certificate.

  1. 01

    Apply

    Tell us who you are and pick the track you want to follow.

  2. 02

    Review

    An administrator reviews every application by hand — no automatic accept.

  3. 03

    Onboard

    Approved applicants get an account and are enrolled into the current cohort.

  4. 04

    Learn, weekly

    Work through your track's lessons and resources at the cohort's pace.

  5. 05

    Build & get reviewed

    Submit each week's practical task; your mentor approves it or asks for a revision.

  6. 06

    Mentorship, throughout

    Weekly huddles and direct feedback keep you on track the whole way.

  7. 07

    Complete & certify

    Meet the completion, attendance and review bar and earn your certificate.

Who it's for

Built for people who learn by doing.

You don't need a degree in the field or a perfect résumé. You need the discipline to show up weekly, submit real work, and take feedback seriously.

  • You're serious about building real, working skill — not just watching videos.
  • You can commit to a weekly cadence for the length of a cohort.
  • You want direct, specific feedback on your work, not just a passing grade.
  • You're comfortable learning alongside a cohort of peers, not in isolation.

The experience

What a week actually looks like.

Monday

A new week unlocks — lessons, resources and that week's practical task, laid out in order.

Through the week

Work the lessons at your own pace, acknowledge resources, and start building your practical task.

Weekly huddle

A live session with your mentor and cohort — discussion, unblocking, and a recorded attendance mark toward your certificate.

Submission

Submit your repository and live demo. Your mentor approves it, or sends it back with feedback to revise and resubmit.

Outcomes

What you leave with.

Skill, demonstrated

A track's worth of completed, reviewed practical work — not just certificates of attendance.

A public portfolio

Your approved projects, publishable on your terms, as evidence of what you can actually do.

A mentor relationship

Weeks of direct, specific feedback from someone who reviewed your actual work, not a rubric bot.

An earned certificate

Issued when you reach 90% required learning completion, 75% huddle attendance, have every required task approved, and receive both your mentor's recommendation and administrator approval. Transparent, and verifiable by anyone with your certificate's code.
